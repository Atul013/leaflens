# LeafLens design

Retain the authored light botanical environment: forest #163c2c, green #246442, lime #d3ecaa, text #253c30, background #f7f9f5 and border #e2e8df. New batch controls use matching green-tinted OKLCH surfaces. DM Sans is the body face; Manrope carries headings. Users inspect photographs on a laptop or phone in ordinary daylight, so the existing bright photograph workspace remains appropriate.

The specimen strip is a contact sheet with image names, progress states and individual remove buttons. An expanded photo sits next to its analysis. The batch summary is a compact comparison table with an explicit inspect action per row, followed by inline observation guidance. Mobile uses stacked results and horizontally scrollable specimen thumbnails, with no page overflow. Motion communicates progress and selection and respects reduced motion. Selected, error, loading and completed states include text, not colour alone.
