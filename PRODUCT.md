# LeafLens

## Register
Product. The existing botanical field station is a research application for examining crop leaf photographs, comparing trained CNN and VGG16 predictions, and reviewing local observations.

## Users and purpose
Students and reviewers need an understandable, usable demonstration with traceable model outputs. The diagnosis workspace must support one to five leaf photographs, a comparison across the selection, and individual attention maps and guidance.

## Brand and principles
Preserve the existing calm green field-station identity, DM Sans and Manrope typography, photographic specimen area and responsive navigation. Prioritise clear task state, honest uncertainty and keyboard access. No fabricated severity, treatment advice or field accuracy. Let image thumbnails and actual results organise the workflow. Selection changes and partial failure must remain recoverable.
